## Nvidia: fiscal Q3 2027 results (17 Nov 2026), guidance, commitments, share price, and the Huang resignation

### Takeaway
Nvidia beat and raised (Q3 revenue $115.8B, +103% y/y; Q4 guide $130.0B +/-3% vs $125.1B consensus; FY2028 growth outlook raised to about 75% from 70%), but guided gross margin down about 3 points to 71-72% and the stock entered the print 35% below its 6 Oct record after the CEO's resignation. The "~$530B" figure is NOT guarantees to customers: it is a third-party sum of all commitments plus guarantees in the July-quarter 10-Q; guarantees themselves were $108.5B (July) and reportedly $118B (October), mainly backing an OpenAI site in Ohio.

### Inferences
- Implied FY2027 revenue is about $423.7B (nine months $293.7B + $130.0B Q4 midpoint), above the $412B pre-print consensus. A 75% FY2028 growth outlook on that base implies about $741B, versus $692B pre-print consensus, so the Street has to move up roughly 7% unless it haircuts management.
- Q4's 14 weeks flatter the sequential growth: $130.0B over 14 weeks is $9.29B per week versus $8.91B per week in Q3 (13 weeks), a 4.2% underlying weekly-rate increase, against 12.2% headline q/q growth. This is the weakest underlying sequential step in the data shown here and is a natural threshold for "decelerating" calls.
- The margin guide (71-72% vs 74.9%) is the first roughly 3-point step-down of the Rubin cycle; management blames ramp costs, but the reported $390B [U] supply-commitment figure tied to memory suggests input-cost inflation is a second driver.
- If the [U] October figures are right, the like-for-like "commitments plus guarantees" total has risen from about $530B to roughly $640B ($390B supply + $102B lease/cloud + $32B neocloud + $118B guarantees). This sum is mine, mixes categories, and needs checking against the 10-Q.
- Kress naming "customers' access to capital" as a guidance input is the first explicit management acknowledgement that financing, not demand, may be the binding constraint.

### Gaps
- The October-quarter 10-Q itself was not located on EDGAR in search; commitment and guarantee figures for 25 Oct rest on CNBC and one Substack. The $390B supply-commitment number is unverified.
- No 18 Nov regular-session close or post-earnings market cap was available.

- Networking revenue is no longer broken out in the release; no figure found.
- Whether there is a formal SEC, DOJ or Commerce investigation of Nvidia is not confirmed in any source read.

## AI revenue and usage: OpenAI, Anthropic, cloud growth and backlog, adoption, tokens, pricing, and the revenue-to-capex ratio

### Takeaway
Reported AI revenue is growing fast but is small against the spend: Anthropic's run-rate was about $81B at 31 Oct (up about $10B in a month) and OpenAI's about $50B at end-September, a combined roughly $131B against about $800B of 2026 hyperscaler capex. Cloud growth accelerated everywhere in Q3 (Google Cloud +103%, Azure +48%, AWS +39%) with about $2.5T of combined backlog, but that backlog is concentrated in a few AI-lab counterparties, and token prices are falling 15-40% a year while volumes rise.

### Inferences
- Revenue-to-capex ratio, strict definition: OpenAI ($50B, end-Sept) + Anthropic ($81B, end-Oct) = about $131B of frontier-lab run-rate against about $800B of five-company 2026 capex, a ratio of about 0.16. On incremental terms the picture is better: Anthropic alone is adding about $10B of run-rate a month.
- Broader definition: latest-quarter cloud revenue annualizes to about $469B (AWS $184B, Azure $133B, Google Cloud $123B, OCI $30B), a ratio of about 0.59 to 2026 capex, but most of that revenue is not AI and part of it is the labs' own spend counted twice.
- Combined backlog is about $2.48T (Microsoft $677B, Oracle $664B, AWS $578B, Google Cloud $561B). It is not independent demand: a large share is OpenAI and Anthropic commitments, so backlog quality depends on the same two revenue lines. Microsoft's RPO going flat q/q ($678B to $677B) is the first stall in the series.
- The Ramp datapoint (volume +37%, spend flat) is the only direct evidence found of a demand-side shortfall in enterprise dollars; it is single-source and should be treated as a hypothesis to test, not a fact.
- The Anthropic IPO on 19 Nov is a live market test of how public investors value the revenue that underwrites the capex; a break below the IPO price is the trigger Reuters' sources named for further pressure on the whole chain.

## Analyst estimates of the capex-to-revenue gap and of when digestion might arrive

### Takeaway
The sell-side base case is still no digestion in 2027: capex consensus is $1.1T-$1.3T (+35-60%) and suppliers guide 2027 up 65-80%. The dissent is recent and specific: SemiAnalysis cut its AI-accelerator capex forecasts by 28% for 2028 and 40% for 2030, Morgan Stanley cut Nvidia's 2027-2028 data-center estimates about 10%, and Bank of America sees hyperscaler free cash flow negative through 2028, which puts the timing of any digestion in the hands of credit markets, with 2028 the year most often implied.

### Cited Findings
- [C] Goldman Sachs: earning a 15% return on invested capital on 2026-2027 capex requires $1.42T of cumulative revenue over 2028-2030; Goldman judges hyperscalers can clear it and locates the debt risk in neoclouds. — [Exencial Substack on Goldman, 16 Nov 2026](https://exencialrp.substack.com/p/goldman-sachs-says-hyperscalers-can)
- [C] Sequoia's David Cahn: roughly $600B annual gap between AI infrastructure spending and the revenue the ecosystem generates. — [Venture Magazine](https://venturemagazine.net/blog/when-ai-capex-overtakes-cash-flow-big-techs-2026-spending-inflection)
- [C] SemiAnalysis (reported 16-17 Nov 2026): cut global AI-accelerator capex forecasts by 28% for 2028 and 40% for 2030, citing slower AI revenue growth, tighter financing and data-center delays. — [24/7 Wall St, 17 Nov 2026](https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/11/17/chip-stocks-are-having-their-worst-two-months-since-2008/)
- [C] Bank of America: hyperscaler aggregate FCF -$64B (2026), -$144B (2027), -$186B (2028). Separate BofA view: debate has shifted to "how much slowdown is already priced in?" — [TFTC](https://www.tftc.io/ai-capex-cash-flow-negative-2027-peak-hyperscalers); [Reuters via Globe and Mail](https://www.theglobeandmail.com/investing/article-shaky-semiconductor-trade-faces-big-week-as-investors-look-for-bottom/)
- [C] Morgan Stanley cut CY2027 and CY2028 Nvidia data-center sales estimates by about 10% after the resignation. — [CNBC, 16 Nov 2026](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/16/nvidia-earnings-jensen-huang-resignation.html)
- [C] One commentary frames 2027 as the expected capex peak. — [TFTC](https://www.tftc.io/ai-capex-cash-flow-negative-2027-peak-hyperscalers)
- [G] Counter-evidence from companies: Amazon expects AWS growth above 40% in 2027 and 2028; SK Hynix expects shortages through 2030; ASML says supply will not meet demand in 2027-2028; Nvidia raised FY2028 growth to 75%. — [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/amazons-aws-growth-hits-39-in-q3-as-capex-guide-jolts-stock); [BigGo](https://finance.biggo.com/news/873d9ecf-d02b-4f2f-99ea-a8169e870b8e); [StockTitan](https://www.stocktitan.net/articles/asml-q3-2026-earnings-ai-chip-machine-demand); [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/17/nvidia-nvda-earnings-report-q3-2027.html)

### Inferences
- Simple gap arithmetic: about $800B of 2026 capex against about $131B of frontier-lab run-rate leaves a gap near $670B, in line with Cahn's roughly $600B. Goldman's hurdle ($1.42T over 2028-2030, about $473B a year) is about 3.6 times today's combined lab run-rate, which is reachable only if the labs keep compounding at close to recent rates through 2028.
- Timing: no published estimate found puts digestion in 2027. The SemiAnalysis cut is back-loaded (2028 and 2030), and BofA's FCF trough is 2028. The implied consensus-dissent window is 2028, with the trigger being financing (spreads, equity issuance, lab IPO reception) rather than orders.
- Three observable early warnings follow from the above: (1) a 2027 capex guide in late January-February that is flat on the Q4 2026 exit rate (about $870B for four companies); (2) hyperscaler bond spreads widening further beyond 119bp; (3) backlog (RPO) declining sequentially at more than one cloud.
