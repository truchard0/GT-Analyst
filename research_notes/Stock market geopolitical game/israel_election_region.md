## 1. Polls, blocs, threshold and Arab parties, who can form a government, and the post-election timeline

### Takeaway
All three final TV polls (released Fri 2026-10-23) put Gadi Eisenkot's Yashar ahead of Likud, 22-23 seats against 19-21. Neither bloc reaches 61. The Netanyahu bloc stands at 50 in all three. The Zionist anti-Netanyahu bloc stands at 52-56. The two Arab-led parties hold 14-16 seats and are the swing bloc, yet Bennett, Lapid and Lieberman refuse to rely on them. The most likely outcomes are a protracted deadlock, a minority government or unity talks, and possibly a repeat election in 2027. Netanyahu stays caretaker prime minister throughout.

**Post-election procedure (standard law; sources are from earlier cycles)**
- The president consults party representatives within about 7 days of the official results. The designated MK gets 28 days, and the president may extend by up to 14.
- If that fails, the president can task another MK. Alternatively, 61 MKs can ask in writing, within 21 days, for a named MK, who then has 14 days.
- If all routes fail, an early election follows within 90 days.
— [Israel Hayom 2019 explainer](https://israelhayom.com/2019/09/18/what-happens-now-a-guide-to-the-perplexed); [Israel Hayom 2021](https://www.israelhayom.com/2021/04/28/president-wont-extend-netanyahus-mandate-to-form-a-government); [vLex/GPO](https://il.vlex.com/vid/president-begins-consultations-for-872258379)

### Inferences
- Indicative calendar (my arithmetic on the rules above, not a published 2026 schedule):
  - 11-04: results certified.
  - About 11-05 to 11-11: Herzog's consultations.
  - About 12-09: first 28-day mandate expires.
  - About 12-23: expiry with the 14-day extension.
  - Late Dec to mid-Jan 2027: a second candidate or the 21-day Knesset route.
  - If everything fails, an election roughly in spring 2027.
- Netanyahu stays caretaker PM through all of this, so a "no result" outcome is a de facto status-quo win for him on war powers in the near term.
- The largest party matters for optics in Herzog's consultations, but the mandate goes to whoever has the most recommendations. On current polls that means neither side cleanly unless Yisrael Beiteinu and Together accept Arab abstention.
- Highest-leverage swing variables:
  - Threshold casualties: the Reservists, Amcha Yisrael and Haredi Public. Their wasted votes shift seats proportionally.
  - Arab turnout.
  - Whether Lieberman and Bennett soften after the vote.
  - Whether Likud MKs defect, as Lieberman claims they would, which is unverified.
- Herzog has openly linked the pardon to unity-government leverage (see section 2). That makes "unity government plus plea deal or pardon" a real tail scenario.

## 2. Netanyahu's trial, the pardon request and Herzog's plea-deal approach, and what winning or losing means for him

### Takeaway
Netanyahu finished his testimony in June 2026, and the trial is now hearing remaining witnesses. Former AG Mandelblit testified starting 2026-10-12. His November 2025 pardon request, made without admitting guilt, is deferred. Since late April 2026 Herzog has pushed a mediated plea deal first. On 2026-09-19 he signalled that the pardon could be leverage toward a unity government. A loss would expose Netanyahu to an Eisenkot-bloc bill barring indicted persons from serving as PM.

### Cited Findings
- **Trial status:**
  - Netanyahu completed his testimony after 98 hearings over about 17-18 months (June 2026).
  - The trial continues with remaining witnesses for "several more months."
  - Former AG Avichai Mandelblit testified starting 2026-10-12.
  — [ToI liveblog](https://www.timesofisrael.com/liveblog_entry/netanyahu-completes-testimony-in-his-corruption-trial-after-98-hearings-stretching-over-nearly-17-months/); [Manila Times/AFP, 2026-06-25](https://www.manilatimes.net/2026/06/25/world/israel-pm-netanyahu-completes-testimony-in-corruption-trials/2372387/amp); [JPost opinion on Mandelblit](https://www.jpost.com/opinion/article-912134) (search summaries)
- **The pardon request:**
  - It was submitted in November 2025 without an admission of guilt.
  - In late April 2026 Herzog deferred a decision, neither granting nor rejecting it, and called for plea-deal talks.
  - The AG and State Prosecutor agreed to talks "provided there are no preconditions, and without detracting from the trial's progress."
  - Herzog's office called an agreement "a proper and correct solution."
  — [Ynet](https://www.ynetnews.com/article/h1auauo6zg); [Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/herzog-pushes-for-new-plea-deal-talks-says-he-wont-consider-netanyahu-pardon-request-yet/); [ToI: AG okays talks](https://www.timesofisrael.com/heeding-herzog-ag-okays-unconditional-talks-for-plea-deal-in-netanyahus-graft-trial/); [Spokesman-Review/AP, 2026-04-26](https://www.spokesman.com/stories/2026/apr/26/israels-president-putting-off-decision-on-pardon-f/)
- **Legal constraints:**
  - The Justice Ministry's Pardons Department said there is no legal basis for a pardon without some admission of guilt, a resignation or a conviction.
  - Any pardon would face High Court review.
  - A plea deal needs the consent of the prosecution and the defence and approval from the court.
  - Trump has repeatedly pressed for a pardon.
  — [Media Line](https://themedialine.org/headlines/herzog-defers-netanyahu-pardon-decision-calls-for-plea-deal-first/); [JPost](https://www.jpost.com/israel-news/politics-and-diplomacy/article-894167) (search summaries)
- **Herzog on 2026-09-19:**
  - His considerations on a pardon as leverage toward a unity government are "solely substantive."
  - He proposed mediation under the President's Residence and wants the trial ended by agreement.
  - "I have a wide set of tools; the discretion is broad."
  - As of that article Netanyahu had not responded to the mediation offer.
  - Herzog aims to avoid another election and cited precedents from 1984 (Chaim Herzog) and 2019 (Rivlin).
  — [JPost, 2026-09-19](https://www.jpost.com/israel-election-2026/article-909097)
- **Stakes for Netanyahu:**
  - FP says he is determined to hold power partly to avoid a conviction.
  - A proposed law barring indicted persons from the premiership would effectively end his career. Lieberman has proposed such a bill and Eisenkot backs one.
  - JPost notes such targeted legislation could face constitutional difficulties.
  — [Foreign Policy](https://foreignpolicy.com/2026/10/20/israel-election-netanyahu-eisenkot-trump-palestinians-gaza/); [JPost](https://www.jpost.com/israel-election-2026/article-912690)
- FP raises a speculative risk that Netanyahu might resist defeat, for example by challenging results. This is the author's opinion, not reporting. — [Foreign Policy](https://foreignpolicy.com/2026/10/20/israel-election-netanyahu-eisenkot-trump-palestinians-gaza/)

### Inferences
- **Win (Netanyahu bloc reaches 61, which no final poll shows):** the trial grinds on, and pressure from Trump and the coalition for a pardon resumes. The judicial-overhaul agenda revives.
- **Deadlock (the base case on polls):** Netanyahu stays caretaker. The pardon or plea deal becomes a bargaining chip in Herzog's unity push, which is the most likely route to a negotiated personal exit.
- **Loss (an Eisenkot-led government):** the bill barring indicted PMs, a 10/7 state inquiry and reversal of the overhaul raise his personal legal exposure. His incentive to bargain for a plea deal or pardon rises sharply.

### Gaps
- I found no October 2026 report on whether Herzog's mediation actually started, stalled or failed. Its status after 09-19 is unknown.
- I found no confirmed date for a verdict, and no report on whether hearings are paused around the election.

## 3. Netanyahu's incentives on Iran around the vote: the planned strike, "not convenient," and security chiefs' warnings

Multiple reports (Channel 12, Axios, then FT on 10-21) say Netanyahu pushed for renewed strikes on Iran before the Israeli and US votes. FT says the IDF was ready to strike the weekend of 10-10. Trump, focused on the 11-03 midterms, said on 10-09 the US would not attack before the midterms. Publicly, Netanyahu said on 10-14 that a pre-vote strike was "not convenient for us." IDF chief Zamir warned that a renewed war could force the election to be postponed. Sources point to strikes possibly resuming after both elections, i.e. from November.

### Inferences
- **Strategic logic, the "game":**

- **Risk window:** the stated US constraint expires after 11-03. Israel's government-formation limbo (11-04 to about late December) coincides with that window. A caretaker Netanyahu retains war powers. Renewed US-Israel strikes in November–December are the scenario sources call "not unreasonable."
- **Election-week tail risk:** Iranian or proxy action against the vote is flagged by Israeli alert levels. Any pre-vote strike would revive the Zamir postponement scenario.

## 4. The Lebanon/Hezbollah front in 2026

### Takeaway
A new Israel–Hezbollah war erupted on 2026-03-02, after the Iran war began. Israel occupies parts of south Lebanon. A temporary ceasefire with Lebanon has held on paper since 04-16, with continued Israeli airstrikes. The June US-brokered framework ties Hezbollah's disarmament to a phased Israeli withdrawal, but Hezbollah rejects it. Military talks in Tampa on 10-20 to 10-22 reportedly ended without agreement. No progress is expected before the Israeli vote.

### Cited Findings
- **Timeline (Wikipedia):**
  - 03-02: Hezbollah fires into northern Israel for the first time since the 2024 ceasefire.
  - 03-04: Israeli troops enter southern towns.
  - 03-16: a larger ground operation begins.
  - 04-08: Netanyahu says the Iran ceasefire does not cover Lebanon, and Israel carries out its largest strikes.
  - 04-16: a 10-day Israel–Lebanon ceasefire, extended repeatedly.
  - 06-03: a US-facilitated agreement, which Hezbollah rejects on 06-04.
  - 06-15: the US–Iran "Islamabad Memorandum" includes a Lebanon ceasefire.
  - 06-19: Trump announces an Israel–Hezbollah truce.
  - 06-26: Rubio announces a framework including full Hezbollah disarmament, which Hezbollah rejects.
  - 07-15/20: Israel withdraws from pilot zones and hands over buffer-zone sections.
  - 07-21: the Lebanese army deploys in Zawtar al-Gharbiyeh.
  - Infobox casualties: Lebanon 4,362 killed and 1.2 million or more displaced; 41 Israeli soldiers and 3 Israeli civilians killed.
  — [Wikipedia: 2026 Lebanon war](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Lebanon_war)
- **House of Commons Library (2026-10-02):** the trilateral Lebanon–Israel–US framework set in June envisages Hezbollah disarming and Israel withdrawing, with no timetable. Implementation has been limited. Hezbollah continues to reject disarmament. — [HoC Library](https://commonslibrary.parliament.uk/research-briefings/cbp-10887/)
- **Tampa talks (10-20 to 10-22):**
  - A Lebanese military delegation pressed for a halt to Israeli attacks (Reuters, 10-17).

Riyadh has twice asked Trump for US strikes; he declined and offers intelligence only. The US ordered non-emergency staff out of Saudi Arabia on 10-21/22. The UK and France sent air defences.
