Convention: **[EXECUTED]** = an action that happened; **[REPORTED PLAN]** = a plan or intention reported by media or officials but not carried out; **[CLAIM]** = an unverified claim by one party; **[UNCONFIRMED]** = something in the brief that I could not confirm.

## 1. Timeline: what happened, Feb 28 to Oct 24 (with corrections to the brief)

### Takeaway
The broad shape in the brief is right. Several dates need correcting:
- The memorandum was **digitally signed 6/14** and formally signed 6/17.
- The ceasefire collapsed on **7/7-8**.
- The "August stand-down" ran from roughly **7/29 to 8/30**.
- "Round 3" began with a **US strike on Larak Island on 8/30** and continued into early September.
- Since early September, neither side has struck major land targets. The war has continued at sea instead, through tanker attacks and the US blockade.
- I found no source for an exact "9/10" start to the pause. "Early September" is the best-supported description.

### Cited Findings

**Opening phase (Feb-Mar)**
- **2/28 [EXECUTED]:** The US and Israel launched hundreds of air strikes across Iran. US codename: Operation Epic Fury. Israeli codename: Operation Roaring Lion. Iran's response was named True Promise IV. The strikes killed Supreme Leader Ali Khamenei and other officials. Wikipedia's section heading reads "27 February – 1 March", so some sources may differ by a day. — [Wikipedia: 2026 Iran war](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Iran_war)
- **3/1:** Iran set up an Interim Leadership Council. **3/8:** Mojtaba Khamenei was elected supreme leader. He was later listed as wounded. **5/21:** he was reported to be in hiding, and IRGC commander Ahmad Vahidi was described as the key formulator of Iran's military and negotiating line. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Iran_war)
- **March:** Iran blocked the Strait of Hormuz and, in late March, claimed sovereignty over it. **3/23:** Trump postponed strikes on Iranian power plants for five days, and Brent fell more than 14% to about $96. **3/26:** he extended the pause to 4/6. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Iran_war); [CNBC 3/23](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/03/23/trump-iran-war-power-plants-energy-infrastructure-middle-east.html); [CNBC 3/26](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/03/26/trump-iran-war-oil-energy-pause.html)

**Ceasefire, talks and dual blockade (Apr-Jun)**
- **4/8:** A two-week ceasefire began. It was strained when Iran resumed its blockade over Israeli strikes in Lebanon. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Iran_war)
- **4/11:** The Islamabad talks lasted 21 hours with no agreement. They were the highest-level US-Iran talks since 1979, and Vance said Iran refused US terms. **4/13 [EXECUTED]:** the US naval blockade of Iranian ports began. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Iran_war)
- **6/8-9:** Israel struck Iran and a US helicopter went down near the strait, after which CENTCOM carried out "self-defense" strikes. **6/14:** Trump announced a deal, a 14-point memorandum, which Vance said was digitally signed that day. Iran said the draft included no production of nuclear weapons and dilution of its enriched stockpile inside Iran. **6/17:** Trump and Pezeshkian signed the "Islamabad Memorandum", which suspended hostilities for 60 days and lifted the dual blockade. **6/18:** CENTCOM lifted the blockade. **6/28:** the US and Iran agreed to stop exchanging attacks. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Iran_war)
- The memorandum guaranteed toll-free passage of Hormuz for 60 days. Tehran said charges could follow once that window closed. — [Lloyd's List](https://www.lloydslist.com/LL1157571/Iran-imposes-mandatory-insurance-on-ships-transiting-Strait-of-Hormuz-with-fees-likely-to-follow); [Middle East Eye](https://www.middleeasteye.net/news/iran-plans-charge-insurance-fee-vessels-transiting-hormuz-after-us-deal-expires)

**Collapse and Round 2 (July)**
- **7/7:** Iran attacked three commercial ships in Hormuz, including a Qatari LNG tanker that was set ablaze. **7/8:** Trump declared the agreement "over". US airstrikes resumed within a day. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Iran_war)
- **Early to mid July:** CENTCOM struck 80 targets, including the Darkhovin nuclear power plant. The IRGC made 85 counterattacks, including on Bahrain and Kuwait. **7/17:** an attack on Muwaffaq Salti Air Base in Jordan killed three US troops. The US reinstated the blockade and withdrew sanctions waivers. CENTCOM says strict blockade enforcement resumed **7/14**. **7/29:** Saudi Arabia publicly joined US strikes on Iran-backed Iraqi militias. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Iran_war); [Fox News live 10/22](https://www.foxnews.com/live-news/us-iran-war-strait-hormuz-oil-prices-10-22-26)

**August lull**
- Active hostilities slowed when the memorandum's 60-day window expired in August. Oman and Qatar ran back-channel diplomacy. The last US strike before the lull was **7/29**. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Iran_war); [Breakthrough News 9/1](https://breakthroughnews.org/2026/09/01/us-resumes-airstrikes-on-iran-after-month-long-pause/)
- **8/13:** Hegseth said the blockade could be kept "indefinitely". **8/15:** Qatar downed an Iranian fighter jet, which Iran confirmed. — [Wikipedia: Iranian strikes on Oman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Iranian_strikes_on_Oman); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Iran_war)

**Round 3 (8/30 to early September)**
- **8/30 [EXECUTED]:** US forces struck IRGC rocket launchers on Larak Island, the first US strike since 7/29. CENTCOM said the aim was to stop IRGC mine-laying. Iran answered with drones on US bases in the UAE and Jordan. — [Breakthrough News](https://breakthroughnews.org/2026/09/01/us-resumes-airstrikes-on-iran-after-month-long-pause/)
- **Early September:** both sides resumed trading airstrikes. The Congressional Research Service said the exchanges approached mid-July levels. — [Reason 9/2](https://reason.com/2026/09/02/strikes-without-end/); [CRS R48887](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/R48887)
- **Since early September:** AFP reported on 10/24 that "neither side has launched major attacks on land-based targets since early September". Activity since then has been tanker attacks and the US blockade. — [AFP via Bangkok Post](https://www.bangkokpost.com/world/3342763/signs-point-to-renewed-major-us-military-ops-against-iran); [BD Pratidin 10/24](https://en.bd-pratidin.com/international/2026/10/24/72341)

**Cost and casualty markers**
- US: 21 killed and 865 injured (Pentagon's revised figures). The DoD put the war's cost at $43.6bn as of September. The CBO estimated at least $38bn, rising about $3bn a month. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Iran_war)
- Iran: 3,528 killed according to the Iranian government; 3,684 according to HRANA. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Iran_war)

### Inferences
- The war has moved from a contest over land targets to one over sea lanes and oil prices. For about seven weeks the decisive "moves" have been tanker strikes, blockade enforcement and the threat of a new strike campaign, not new strikes on land.
- The pattern so far: each pause has been tied to a diplomatic deadline (the 4/8 two-week ceasefire, the 6/17 memorandum's 60-day window). Each pause collapsed through incidents at sea (4/12-13, 7/7). The 11/3 midterms are the current deadline that holds the pause in place.

### Gaps
- I found no source dating a fire pause to exactly "9/10", and no clean end date for Round 3. "Early September" is the best-supported wording.
- The full 14 points of the memorandum were not retrieved.

## 2. Strait of Hormuz: volumes, the attack campaign, the blockade, tolls, insurance and control

### Takeaway
Two campaigns are running at once:
- **Iran** is attacking Gulf-state tankers. It uses mines, projectiles and spotters in small boats, and claims to "manage" the strait.
- **The US** is blockading Iranian ports while escorting Gulf-state tankers through.

October 2026 is the worst month of the war for ship attacks: 27 by IMO count as of 10/22, and Iran claimed 7 ships hit on 10/22 alone. Even so, Gulf (non-Iranian) export volumes have partly or largely recovered under escort. The two sides are fighting over the numbers: Bessent and Trump say flows are above pre-war levels, while ship counts remain a fraction of pre-war traffic.

### Cited Findings

**Volumes**
- Pre-war, about 125 large commercial vessels a day used the strait, carrying about 17.1m barrels a day, roughly 20% of global crude and LNG. In September, Gulf oil flows through Hormuz excluding Iran recovered to more than 81% of pre-war levels (Kpler). Daily flows: about 20.8m bbl on 10/8 and 2.9m bbl on 10/11, the lowest of October. CENTCOM said it helped ship more than 19m bbl on 10/12. — [Al Jazeera 10/14](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/14/iran-steps-up-hormuz-surveillance-attacks-soar-oil-flows-crash-again)
- From 9/28 to about 10/4, ship transits averaged fewer than 23 a day (MarineTraffic). Moving a VLCC from the US to Asia cost about $77m, against $9.2m on average last year (Baltic Exchange via Bloomberg). — [NBC News 10/8](https://www.nbcnews.com/business/energy/oil-prices-stocks-trump-iran-rcna602248)
- Transits from 10/5 to 10/11 were 31% above August's weekly average (Lloyd's List). — [CNBC 10/9 via search summary](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/09/iran-war-strait-hormuz-tanker-attack-oil.html)
- **10/22 [CLAIM]:** Trump said at the Sioux City rally that more oil moved "over the last week than at any time previous", crediting US escorts. **10/24 [CLAIM]:** Bessent said Persian Gulf oil exports had surpassed pre-war levels. — [Fox live 10/22](https://www.foxnews.com/live-news/us-iran-war-strait-hormuz-oil-prices-10-22-26); [Iran International liveblog](https://www.iranintl.com/en/liveblog/202610187555)
- Iraq said its October exports averaged more than 3.4m b/d across Hormuz, Ceyhan and trucks through Syria (government claim). Basra exports were about 2.4-2.6m b/d in September. — [Sunday Guardian](https://sundayguardianlive.com/world/us-iran-war-latest-live-news-iraq-oil-exports-surge-above-34-million-barrels-per-day-in-october-as-baghdad-uses-strait-of-hormuz-turkey-and-syria-routes-308857/); [Washington Institute](https://www.washingtoninstitute.org/policy-analysis/iraqs-energy-challenges-amid-hormuz-crisis)

**Iran's attack campaign (Sept-Oct)**
- 9/28 to 10/4: Kpler counted 10 tankers struck, plus one more on 10/5. Wikipedia counts at least 12 IRGC attacks that week, the highest weekly count of the war. — [Al Jazeera 10/14](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/14/iran-steps-up-hormuz-surveillance-attacks-soar-oil-flows-crash-again); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Iran_war)
- 10/6 to 10/14: UKMTO logged 15 attacks, 11 of them in the last four days. On 10/11, JMIC rated the threat "severe". — [Al Jazeera 10/14](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/14/iran-steps-up-hormuz-surveillance-attacks-soar-oil-flows-crash-again)
- 10/11 to 10/16: Iran targeted at least 12 commercial ships. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Iran_war)
- About 10/14: a container ship sank, the **third commercial ship sunk** in the war (IMO). The IRGC said it hit a mine. An earlier sinking was the Indian-flagged *Haji Ali* off Oman on 5/13. — [CNBC 10/14](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/14/container-ship-sinks-strait-hormuz-as-iran-war-attacks.html); [Wikipedia: Iranian strikes on Oman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Iranian_strikes_on_Oman)
- **As of 10/22:** the IMO counted 27 attacks on commercial ships in October, the most of any month in the war. USNI notes that the US has also disabled some ships as part of its blockade. — [USNI 10/22 (via search summary; page returned 403)](https://news.usni.org/2026/10/22/october-marks-highest-number-of-strait-of-hormuz-attacks)
- A Reuters review found more than 30 VLCCs damaged in the war, 11 of them in October. — [search summary citing Reuters; IBTimes AU](https://www.ibtimes.com.au/strait-hormuz-ship-attacks-record-oil-traffic-1877334)
- **10/22 [CLAIM]:** the IRGC said it struck four ships doing ship-to-ship oil transfers in the northern-central Gulf, and that three more hit mines west of the strait. The IRGC also warned the conflict "will extend" to US bases if US escorts continue. **10/22-23:** Trump said Iran was "making up fake hits, mines, and everything else". — [Saudi Gazette](https://saudigazette.com.sa/article/665644/world/irans-irgc-threatens-us-bases-over-hormuz-escorts-claims-strikes-on-7-ships); [Fox live](https://www.foxnews.com/live-news/us-iran-war-strait-hormuz-oil-prices-10-22-26); [JPost 10/24](https://www.jpost.com/international/article-912674)
- Iran's methods: on 10/13 the Navy deployed a new surveillance formation east of Hormuz, across the Sea of Oman and the Arabian Sea. It told ships to keep transponders on and to coordinate passage with Iran. A US official said on 10/14 that Iran's radar was largely destroyed, so the IRGC relies on unarmed spotters in dhows. The US destroyed one such boat on 10/6. — [Al Jazeera 10/14](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/14/iran-steps-up-hormuz-surveillance-attacks-soar-oil-flows-crash-again); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Iran_war)

On 10/23 a tanker spilled oil off Umm Al Quwain, cause unidentified. — [Euronews 10/8](https://www.euronews.com/2026/10/08/iran-strikes-tankers-outside-hormuz-in-signs-of-expanding-campaign); [GV Wire/Reuters 10/23](https://gvwire.com/2026/10/23/oil-prices-jump-3-on-houthi-attacks-oil-spill-highest-settlement-since-april)

**The US blockade and escorts**
- The blockade of Iranian ports began 4/13, was lifted 6/18 and reinstated in July (strict enforcement from 7/14). On 10/16 and 10/22 (its 100th day), CENTCOM said it had stopped at least **$25bn** in Iranian crude exports. CENTCOM and Treasury froze about $350m of Iranian crypto. Iran has loaded no crude for export since late August. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Iran_war); [Fox live 10/22](https://www.foxnews.com/live-news/us-iran-war-strait-hormuz-oil-prices-10-22-26); [CNBC 10/6 via search summary](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/06/crude-oil-tanker-strait-hormuz-iran-attack.html)

**Tolls, fees and Oman**
- **5/28:** Trump threatened to "blow up" Oman over reported Iran-Oman talks on a joint toll. **8/6:** Oman accepted a framework with Iran to temporarily reopen the strait. **8/17:** Iran said it had a deal with Oman. **8/18:** Kpler said Iran had partly lost control of the strait, because ships were using the UN-authorized Omani route and could not be tolled. **8/25:** the US said mines were cleared from the Omani-side lanes. **8/26:** the IRGC claimed agreements with Oman on "share and revenue", which Oman has not confirmed. — [Wikipedia: Iranian strikes on Oman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Iranian_strikes_on_Oman); [straits.live](https://straits.live/strait-of-hormuz-transit-fee)
- Iran's Persian Gulf Strait Authority requires ships to carry Iran-approved insurance, with Iran paying the premium for now. Iran rejects the word "toll" but says "services" require "fees". Reported charges run up to $2m per voyage, with no published tariff. The US opposes any charging regime controlled by Iran. — [Lloyd's List](https://www.lloydslist.com/LL1157571/Iran-imposes-mandatory-insurance-on-ships-transiting-Strait-of-Hormuz-with-fees-likely-to-follow); [Newsweek](https://www.newsweek.com/did-iran-just-get-tolls-for-strait-hormuz-12090257); [Forbes 7/28](https://www.forbes.com/sites/guneyyildiz/2026/07/28/hormuz-is-becoming-a-fee-and-permission-regime/)

**Insurance and freight**
- War-risk premiums were 3-8% of hull value before the June ceasefire, with trade-press figures up to about $10m per transit. The US government has acted as insurer of last resort. — [straits.live / search summary](https://straits.live/strait-of-hormuz-transit-fee)
- In September, VLCC day rates on Gulf-China routes exceeded $1.2m (FT). Crews receive large bonuses. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Iran_war); [Al Jazeera 10/14](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/14/iran-steps-up-hormuz-surveillance-attacks-soar-oil-flows-crash-again)

**Who controls what**
- **US and partners:** the sea lanes on the Omani side and the escort corridor, plus a full blockade of Iran's ports.
- **Iran:** a deniable harassment capability using mines, projectiles and drones. It claims to "manage" the strait (Rezaei: "the strait is under Iran's control"). On 10/24, Iran's armed forces chief Abdollahi said Iran would not allow US warships into the Persian Gulf "under any circumstances". — [Al Jazeera 10/14](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/14/iran-steps-up-hormuz-surveillance-attacks-soar-oil-flows-crash-again); [Iran International](https://www.iranintl.com/en/liveblog/202610187555)

**Oil prices**
- 10/8: Brent closed at $104.28 and WTI at $91.49. US retail gasoline was $4.36/gal (more than 45% above 2/28) and diesel $6.28 (up about 70%). 10/13: Brent $105.69. 10/14: Brent $106.37 intraday. — [NBC 10/8](https://www.nbcnews.com/business/energy/oil-prices-stocks-trump-iran-rcna602248); [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/14/iran-steps-up-hormuz-surveillance-attacks-soar-oil-flows-crash-again); [OilPrice 10/14](https://oilprice.com/Latest-Energy-News/World-News/Oil-Prices-Climb-Above-106-as-Iran-Threatens-More-Tanker-Attacks.html)

The drivers were Houthi attacks in Saudi Arabia, the Aramco HQ evacuation, the oil spill off the UAE, and Iran's claim of 7 ships hit. The SPR is at its lowest since 1982, and the DOE authorized a 400k-bbl SPR exchange. — [GV Wire/Reuters 10/23](https://gvwire.com/2026/10/23/oil-prices-jump-3-on-houthi-attacks-oil-spill-highest-settlement-since-april)
- Bank of America (Blanch, early September): Brent $95-120 if disruption lasts through year-end, and up to $150 if energy infrastructure is badly damaged. — [NBC 10/8](https://www.nbcnews.com/business/energy/oil-prices-stocks-trump-iran-rcna602248)

### Inferences
- Volumes can recover while attacks rise, because the escort corridor carries large daily cargoes. The marginal price driver is therefore risk premium (insurance, freight, headlines about attacks) more than lost barrels. That explains why Brent reached a six-month high in the same week Bessent claimed exports were above pre-war levels.
- Iran shifted tactics around 10/4: more mines, ship-to-ship transfer targets, and attacks outside the strait. This attacks the US "flows are fine" narrative directly. The 10/22-23 dispute over "fake hits" is itself part of the game, with each side trying to move the oil market through the information war.

### Gaps
- USNI and the Washington Post returned 403 errors, so their detail is taken from search summaries.
- I found no current (October) Kpler, Vortexa or Windward daily flow series, and no current official war-risk premium.
- Whether any Omani or Iranian fee is actually being collected as of October is **unconfirmed**.
- "12+ attacks a week" is supported for 9/28-10/4 and 10/11-16, but I have no clean weekly series beyond those weeks.

## 3. Washington and Jerusalem: the midterm pledge, post-midterm strike plans, the blocked Netanyahu offensive

### Takeaway
On **10/8**, Trump publicly ruled out attacking Iran before the 11/3 midterms. Multiple outlets now report **[REPORTED PLAN]** that US and Israeli militaries have drawn up a "short, intense" post-midterm strike campaign against energy, nuclear (including Pickaxe Mountain) and missile/drone targets.
- No order has been given.
- The US build-up is due to finish in early November, and a third carrier is expected at the end of November.
- On **10/21**, the FT reported that Trump blocked a Netanyahu offensive planned for the weekend of 10/10. Netanyahu denies this.
- I could **not** confirm a specific 10/22 Trump statement "postponing major combat to avoid energy-price fallout". What I did find:
  - an NYT report that Trump has repeatedly vetoed a return to major combat and wants targeting to avoid refineries to protect gasoline prices;
  - his 10/22 remark that the war "may carry a price to pay" politically and that the US may hit Iran "a little bit further";
  - his 10/23 remark "We may do another little Epic Fury."

### Cited Findings
- **10/8 [EXECUTED statement]:** Trump on Truth Social: "We will not be attacking Iran at any time prior to the Midterm Elections," citing "productive discussions with the Islamic Republic of Iran." It followed NBC reports that he was weighing strikes before the vote. Oil largely ignored it: Brent closed at $104.28 (+4%) that day and diesel futures rose 6%. — [NBC 10/8](https://www.nbcnews.com/business/energy/oil-prices-stocks-trump-iran-rcna602248)
- **Early October [REPORTED]:** the US and Israel came close to ordering new strikes, and Trump decided against them at the last minute (NYT). — [JPost/Reuters 10/24](https://www.jpost.com/international/article-912674); [IBTimes UK](https://www.ibtimes.co.uk/us-israel-plans-possible-iran-strikes-after-midterms-1827372)

## 4. Iran's strategy, conditions for talks, and nuclear status

### Takeaway
According to its own officials, Tehran is deliberately using the tanker war to keep oil prices high and make the war costly for Trump before 11/3. It expects Trump to be less sensitive to prices after the vote, so it is front-loading the pain.
- Its terms, passed through Qatar and Oman: end the war on all fronts, release frozen assets, lift the naval blockade. These expanded to seven conditions by mid-October.
- Trump rejected Iran's seven-day ceasefire proposal on 9/26.
- On the nuclear side, the IAEA board referred Iran to the UN Security Council on 9/9. An NPT-withdrawal bill was filed on 9/20 but has not been enacted. Iran insists it has not abandoned safeguards.

Iran is now pressuring the Gulf on two fronts.
- **Hormuz:** Iran's own tanker attacks.
- **Bab el-Mandeb:** the Houthis seized Mokha and Perim Island around 9/10-11 and are bombarding Saudi cities and airports.

Saudi Arabia is now a direct front-line target: airports in Riyadh and Dammam have been hit, Aramco's HQ evacuated, and US staff ordered out. Qatar and Oman serve as mediators. Iraq is exporting through a mix of routes.

## 6. Plausible next moves, Nov-Dec 2026: triggers and deadlines

### Takeaway
This section is analysis built on the cited facts above, not reported fact. Key dates:
- **10/27:** Israeli election; Pezeshkian meets Putin in Moscow.
- **11/3:** US midterms, the end of Trump's pledge.
- **Early November:** US build-up complete.
- **End of November:** third carrier expected.

The base case implied by the reporting is that the land war stays paused until 11/3, followed by elevated risk of a "short, intense" US/Israeli round from mid-November unless a deal emerges through the Qatar channel.

### Cited Findings (deadlines and triggers)
- Trump: no attack "at any time prior to the Midterm Elections" (10/8). — [NBC](https://www.nbcnews.com/business/energy/oil-prices-stocks-trump-iran-rcna602248)
- US build-up complete by early November; Trump has not signed off; strikes would be short and intense and would avoid refineries. — [IBTimes UK/NYT](https://www.ibtimes.co.uk/us-israel-plans-possible-iran-strikes-after-midterms-1827372)

- Israeli election 10/27. A caretaker government would make launching a war harder. — [IBTimes UK](https://www.ibtimes.co.uk/us-israel-plans-possible-iran-strikes-after-midterms-1827372)

### Inferences (player by player)

**Trump / US**
- Wants lower gasoline prices into 11/3 and a "win" (a deal, or a short decisive strike) afterwards.
- Fears an oil spike from hitting refineries, US casualties, depleted interceptor and missile stocks, and a 60% disapproval rating.
- **Options:**
  - keep the blockade, escorts and sanctions (Economic Outcast);
  - strike the Houthis before 11/3;
  - launch a short post-midterm campaign against energy, nuclear (Pickaxe) and missile targets;
  - accept a deal through Qatar trading blockade relief and frozen assets for an end to tanker attacks.
- **Triggers for strikes:** mass-casualty attacks on US forces, a ship sunk under escort, Iranian moves on the NPT, or the build-up completing with talks deadlocked.

**Iran**
- Wants the blockade lifted, frozen assets released, an end to the war on all fronts, and regime survival.
- Fears a post-midterm strike package, total export loss, and economic collapse.
- **Options:**
  - intensify the tanker and mine war before 11/3;
  - use the Houthis against Saudi Arabia;
  - pass the NPT-withdrawal bill;
  - strike Gulf infrastructure, cables and data centres if attacked;
  - make a de-escalation offer after the vote to head off strikes.
- **Triggers:** US escorts entering the Gulf, strikes on nuclear sites, the US joining the Houthi war.

**Netanyahu / Israel**
- Wants regime change and the end of the nuclear and missile threat.
- Constrained by Trump's veto and by the 10/27 election.
- **Options:** a joint restart "in the coming weeks" (FT source); unilateral action is unlikely while Trump objects.

**Saudi Arabia / UAE / Qatar / Oman**
- Want the war ended and Hormuz reopened (10/24 joint call), along with US protection.
- Fear Iranian and Houthi strikes on airports, Aramco and LNG.
- **Options:** mediation (Qatar, Oman), the Saudi-backed Yemeni counteroffensive (since 10/4), Pakistan troops, and lobbying Washington against strikes on energy targets.

**Houthis**
- Hold Bab el-Mandeb and are escalating against Saudi Arabia.
- **Trigger for wider action:** US entry into the Saudi campaign.

**Market-relevant signposts**
- IMO/UKMTO weekly attack counts.
- CENTCOM blockade and escort statements.
- Brent's $117 area (the 10/23 close of $116.72 was a six-month high).
- Whether Trump signs off on the NYT-reported plan after 11/3.
- The 10/27 Israeli result.
- Any Qatar-channel announcement.
