## The core game (why the forecast splits the way it does)
- **Chessboard (§4).** Iran's *king* is physically decapitated. Hybrid theocracy → **both** failure modes at once: succession crisis (autocracy) **and** factional/extremist splintering (theocracy). Mojtaba's invisibility is the onset.
- **Two-level game (formal §5).** The regime's international move (accept the deal) is outside its **domestic win-set** (a base demanding vengeance). Leaders bridge that gap with *controlled* retaliation and defiance rhetoric — not by honoring the peace cleanly.
- **Fearon commitment problem (formal §2).** No one can credibly commit for a leaderless Iran → the deal is an armistice, not a settlement.
- **Allies separate (§4).** Israel's optimal play diverges: a *stable* US-brokered Iran is a threat to it, so it has standing incentive to probe below the war line. Erdogan's warning confirms the risk is live.
- **The honest fork.** Decapitation may equally have produced **capitulation** (regime too broken to fight → quiet). That's why near-term calls lean "ceasefire holds" while structural calls lean "no durable peace."

### The synthesis (base case)
The most likely 60-day path: **the state stays intact, the ceasefire broadly holds at war-scale, but "peace" decentralizes into probes.** Mojtaba governs from the shadows with the IRGC holding real power (#18); the enraged base is placated with defiance rhetoric and at least one controlled retaliation (#20); Israel conducts a limited below-war-line strike (#21) that strains but does not detonate the deal (#19); Hormuz keeps grinding back open but doesn't fully normalize (#22); oil stays capped/soft (#23). Note #19 and #21 are **compatible by design** — a targeted Israeli strike or an Iranian proxy hit is *below* the "war-scale resumption" threshold that would falsify #19.

### The two tails
- **Downside (chaos, ~15–20%):** succession fight turns violent / elite defection → the #17 low-probability collapse leg; would spike crude and defense, crater the glut trade.
- **Upside (clean capitulation, ~15%):** IRGC installs Mojtaba cleanly, proxies stand down, deal holds — this is the world where #10/#18/#20 all *fail*. Watch for a confident public Mojtaba address + a Houthi stand-down as the tell.
