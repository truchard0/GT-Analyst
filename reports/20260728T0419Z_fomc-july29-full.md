## WHY THE FED HOLDS TOMORROW DESPITE OIL AT $132 (the fade, five reasons)
1. **It's not in the data.** June CPI (−0.4%) and PPI (−0.3%) were *soft* — the oil spike is a July event that hits the **August 12 CPI**, after this meeting. The Fed reacts to prints, not headlines.
2. **No SEP.** Major policy pivots almost always ship with a Summary of Economic Projections. July has none — the natural meeting to *signal* and hike in September (which does have an SEP), not to move now.
3. **§11 supply-shock look-through.** The textbook central-bank response to an oil supply shock is to look through it unless it de-anchors long-run expectations. Hiking into oil-driven inflation *tightens into a real-income hit* — the classic policy error the Fed knows to avoid.
4. **Financial-stability caution mid-crisis.** Hiking during an acute geopolitical shock (war round 2, a second front, equities wobbling) is pro-cyclical risk the Fed usually declines in real time.
5. **Optionality.** Hold + hawkish guidance keeps every card; an actual hike *spends* the coercive threat. A reputation-building chair (§12.13) prefers to hold the loaded gun visible on the table.
