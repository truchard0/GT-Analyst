## PART 2 — Game-theoretic read

### Classic frame
- **Chessboard (§4):** the *king* — Iran's theocratic political system — was physically decapitated. A hybrid theocracy fails through **both** succession crisis *and* factional splintering. Mojtaba's invisibility is the onset.
- **Fearon commitment problem (formal §2):** no one can credibly commit for a leaderless Iran (who signs? the heir won't appear), so the deal is structurally an armistice.
- **Allies separate (§4):** Israel's optimal play diverges — a *stable* US-brokered Iran is a threat to it, giving it a standing incentive to probe below the war line.

### New modern-GT lenses (§12 — first live use)
- **§12.6 Propaganda under censorship is uninformative.** The "mass grief / defiance / new regional order / vengeance" state coverage is exactly the censorship+propaganda case — its informational content is **zero by design**. **Do not read regime strength from the funeral optics.** The genuinely informative datum is the one they *couldn't* stage away: **Mojtaba's absence.** Read the censorship apparatus, not the broadcast.
- **§12.1/§12.2 Regime survival is a global game.** Collapse isn't "indeterminate" — it has a **threshold θ\***. Decapitation + succession vacuum **lowers the fundamental** toward θ*. The tail risk is a *coordination tipping* problem: it triggers if a **single precise public signal** becomes common knowledge among elites (a visible IRGC split, a failed Mojtaba appearance, a mass-casualty event) — the **destabilizing-public-information** channel. So the collapse tail is *low-probability but sharply state-dependent*, not a slow drift.
- **§12.8 Common-knowledge, not narrative, is the trigger.** The shared "vengeance" mood alone won't topple the regime; what matters is whether **elites become uncertain about what other elites will do**. Watch elite behavior (defections, no-shows, purges), not street sentiment.
- **§12.4 Cheap-talk coarseness.** Erdogan's warning, Iran's vengeance rhetoric, US reassurances — all biased communicators. Extract only a **coarse bucket** (direction of alignment), not precise intent.
- **§12.7 Reputation ≠ commitment.** The "peace deal" rests on a revocable General License and announcements, not an observable irreversible commitment → discount its durability (this is the formal spine under ledger **#10**).

- **Chaos tail (~15–20%):** succession fight turns violent / elite defection cascades past θ* → the low-probability **regime-collapse** leg (#17 inverse). **Trigger to watch:** a precise, common-knowledge public signal (IRGC split, failed Mojtaba appearance).
